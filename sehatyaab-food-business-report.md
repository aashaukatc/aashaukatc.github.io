# Sehatyaab Healthy Food Business Research

## Working Thesis

A healthy home-made pre-order food business in Rawalpindi and Islamabad should not launch as a generic healthy-food brand. The stronger direction is a small-batch, direct-order-first brand with three clear product lines:

1. High-protein meals and snacks for gym-goers.
2. Safe, lunchbox-friendly products for mothers buying for children.
3. Portion-controlled, visually appealing low-carb or balanced products for women focused on wellness, energy, skin, figure, and daily nutrition.

## Core Positioning

The safest and strongest early positioning is:

> Fresh, small-batch, traceable ingredients; organic where documented.

This avoids overclaiming around “organic” while still keeping the brand premium, health-conscious, and trust-led.

## Launch Logic

| Segment | Product Angle | Channel |

## Operating Model

The business should be pre-order first, not open-ended on-demand. The operational sequence should be:

```text
Instagram / WhatsApp / Web Form
-> Order Cutoff
-> Demand Consolidation
-> Procurement + Prep Sheet
-> Batch Cooking
-> QC + Labeling + Allergen Check
-> Route-Based Dispatch
-> Feedback + Repeat Order Prompt
```

## Compliance Notes

Before commercial packaging, product claims should be validated. Strong claims such as organic, detox, immunity-boosting, PCOS-friendly, beauty-enhancing, or medical-style nutrition claims should not be used loosely. Safer launch language should prioritize freshness, traceability, ingredients, preparation date, consume-by date, allergen visibility, and transparent nutrition estimates.

## Portfolio Role

Sehatyaab is currently best presented as a healthy food / nutrition product lab inside the broader Aftab Shaukat portfolio: a practical example of market research, product positioning, consumer segmentation, brand asset development, pricing architecture, and direct-order operating design.
