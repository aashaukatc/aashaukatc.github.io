About Muhammad Aftab Shaukat

I connect healthcare operations
with systems that work.

Senior revenue-cycle and operations professional, business analyst and startup builder based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan.

Operating philosophy

Understand the business.
Then build the system.

My career developed through direct work with U.S. healthcare billing, A/R, claims, denials, payer operations, revenue integrity and delivery teams. That ground-level experience shaped how I approach analytics, process architecture and technology: find what actually prevents the work from moving.

I work at the intersection of operational delivery and systems design—translating business requirements into actionable workflows, measuring performance, and helping teams adopt the changes they need.

Beyond healthcare, I explore products and ventures involving data, civic information, nutrition commerce and AI-enabled workflows. These are distinct projects at different stages, not claims of commercial scale.
